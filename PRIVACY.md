# SnapCull Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 15 November 2026_

SnapCull is a photo culling app for Windows made by Neel Gupta. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

## The short version

**SnapCull does not collect, send or share any personal information.** It works entirely on your own computer. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no internet connection.

## What SnapCull does with your files

- **Your photos** are only read from folders you choose to open. They are never uploaded or copied anywhere, except to a folder you pick yourself with *Copy / move picks* or *Convert*. When converting, you choose whether camera details and GPS location are kept in the new files.
- **Your ratings** (picks, rejects, stars and colour labels) are saved in `.xmp` sidecar files next to your photos, in the same folders. Nothing is stored anywhere else.
- **Your settings** (for example the last folder you opened, and whether the histogram is shown) are saved in a small file on your PC at `%AppData%\SnapCull\settings.json`. They never leave your computer.
- **Photo details** such as camera model, lens, date and GPS location stay inside your photo files. SnapCull only displays them on your screen and never sends them anywhere.

Uninstalling SnapCull removes the app. Your photos and `.xmp` sidecar files stay where they are, because they belong to you.

## Internet access

SnapCull does not connect to the internet. It contains no code that sends data to Neel Gupta or anyone else.

## The Microsoft Store

If you install SnapCull from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft may collect information about the download, installation and app crashes as described in the [Microsoft Privacy Statement](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement). Neel Gupta only receives the anonymous, combined statistics that Microsoft provides to app publishers, such as the number of installs.

## Children

SnapCull does not collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published here with a new date. SnapCull is open source, so you can check what it does in the code at <https://github.com/neel-gupta-dev/SnapCull>.
